# Build-Log Post Template

For Days 22–23. Stealth rules from your LinkedIn skill apply: practitioner framing, never "I'm job hunting," never "excited to announce." One real number beats ten polished lines.

## Structure
**Hook (1–2 lines):** drop into the problem or a surprising result. No throat-clearing.
> e.g. "Intake-to-plan used to take my team an afternoon. I rebuilt it as something that runs in about a minute."

**Body (3–6 short paragraphs, lots of white space):**
- The real problem (specific, from your world).
- What you built — plainly. Name the system.
- One honest surprise or lesson (what broke, what was easier than expected).
- The outcome, with a number if you have one.

**Ending:** a genuine question to peers, or an honest reflection. No forced CTA.

**Hashtags:** 3–5, lowercase, at the very end.
> #marketingops #ai #automation #n8n

## Hard avoids (from your skill)
- "Excited to share" / "Humbled to announce" / "Let's connect!"
- Humble-brags disguised as lessons
- Sharing the build without your actual take on it

## Before posting
Read it out loud. If it doesn't sound like you talking, rewrite it.
