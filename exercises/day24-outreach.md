# Day 24 — Outreach

Goal: start the conversations. You have a portfolio, two posts, and talking points — now use them.

## Pick your targets
1. Identify **3–5** people to connect with: applied-AI / automation / cross-functional leaders whose work overlaps yours.
2. And/or line up **2–3 exploration chats** about your own practice (potential clients/partners).

## Reach out (stealth, practitioner-led)
3. Use the **LinkedIn skill** to draft connection notes. Lead with the work, not the ask: reference something specific about them + a relevant build of yours. Never "I'm job hunting."
4. For exploration chats: "I've been building AI automations/connectors — would love to compare notes on where this is useful." Curiosity, not a pitch.
5. Send them. Link your portfolio where it fits naturally.

## Log it
6. Record each request/chat (who, date, angle) — track responses so you can follow up.

## ✅ Proof
Sent requests/chats, logged. Add to the tracker.

## Why it feeds the work
Pipeline for both paths (JOB + EXPLORE). The builds were the hard part; this is where they pay off.
