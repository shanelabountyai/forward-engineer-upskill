# Day 18 — Quantify the impact

Goal: put numbers on the case study — your signature move. "$10M eliminated, 400% efficiency" is your brand; apply that rigor here.

Adds an **Impact** section to `case-study-n8n.md`.

## Build the estimate
1. **Time per use:** how long the manual version takes (intake → drafting a plan → logging → notifying) vs. the workflow. Be conservative and state your assumptions.
2. **Error/consistency:** what the automation makes reliable that humans get wrong or skip.
3. **At scale:** model it at volume — e.g. 1,000+ requests/year. Hours saved = (manual minutes − automated minutes) × volume.
4. Translate hours → cost using a defensible loaded rate (state it).

## Keep it defensible
5. Every number gets an assumption next to it. "If X requests at Y minutes saved each…" — a skeptic should be able to follow the math. Defensible beats impressive.
6. Add a one-line **range** (conservative → optimistic) rather than a single hero number.
